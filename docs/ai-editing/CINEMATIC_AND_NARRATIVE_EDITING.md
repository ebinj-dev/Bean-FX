# Cinematic And Narrative Editing (M42-C)

Recipes: see STYLE_CATALOG.md / scripts/ai-editing/style-recipes.json (cinematic-trailer, cinematic-storytelling).

Cinematic cutting lives and dies on motion continuity: cut at motion peaks, keep screen direction stable, and let establishing shots breathe (>= 2 s). Push-ins (scale 1.0-1.08, EASE_IN_OUT) replace static holds.

Trailer structure: hook (first 8%), rising stakes, accelerating montage, climax + title. Acts accelerate monotonically; through-black fades mark act breaks; the longest hold sits in the quiet beat before the climax.

Color: shot-matched contrast/saturation ranges, never a canned LUT. Protect skin tones after any grade.

**Execution contract:** everything described maps to real editor capabilities or is disclosed via the capability matrix (STYLE_CAPABILITY_MATRIX.md). Nothing in this file instructs RIISHE to fake an unsupported effect.
